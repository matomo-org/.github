# Matomo
**The leading open-source web analytics platform that gives you 100% data ownership.**

![](https://matomo.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Dasboard-01-01.png)

## Why is this so awesome? 🤩

* 📊 **Full Analytics Power** - Track, analyse and understand your visitors’ behaviour with complete flexibility and depth.
* 🕵️‍♂️ **Respect Privacy** - Comply with privacy laws like GDPR, HIPAA, and CCPA, while keeping full control over your users’ data.
* 🔐 **Own Your Data** - Your data stays with you. No one else can access it - not even us.
* 🚀 **Customisable and Extensible** - Extend Matomo with [more than one hundred plugins](https://plugins.matomo.org/) from Heatmaps to Funnels.
* 💻 **Open Source** - Transparent, auditable, and community-driven. Always free from vendor lock-in.
* 🔒 **Security** with [HackerOne bounty program](https://hackerone.com/matomo), two-factor authentication, and Enterprise security capabilities.

Want to see what Matomo can do? [**Learn about all our features**](https://matomo.org/product-features/).

## Get your Matomo 🚚

- ☁️ [**Matomo Cloud**](https://matomo.org/matomo-cloud/) - Fully managed by the Matomo team. Hassle-free setup, hosting, and updates.
- 🏠 [**Matomo On-Premise**](https://matomo.org/download/) - Download and host Matomo yourself for total data ownership.
- 🔌 [**Matomo for WordPress**](https://wordpress.org/plugins/matomo/) - Analytics built right into your WordPress site.
- 🏢 Looking for enterprise-grade support? Discover [**Matomo Enterprise**](https://matomo.org/enterprise/).
